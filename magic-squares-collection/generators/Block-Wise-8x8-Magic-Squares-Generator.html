Options

One generator combines all 8x8 block structures, with a border line around every verified concentric level. Each larger square uses 1 through n*n exactly once. The selector identifies equal or different block sums and whether the complete square is pandiagonal. Every selected border, core and inner block is verified.

Custom orders use the same Block structure selector above, including every division available in the presets. Supported multiples extend through 1000.

Magic Square 8x8 Pandiagonal

Block and inner-structure verification

Every selected block, core and inner division is verified according to its stated property. The complete square is also checked for equal rows, columns and both main diagonals.
