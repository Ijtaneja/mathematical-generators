Multiples of 10 - 72 verified choices through order 108 - Combined block structures

Options

One generator combines all 10x10 block structures. Each larger square uses 1 through n*n exactly once. The selector identifies equal or different block sums and whether the complete square is pandiagonal. Every selected border, core and inner block is verified.

Magic Square 8x8 Pandiagonal

Block and inner-structure verification

Every selected block, core and inner division is verified according to its stated property. The complete square is also checked for equal rows, columns and both main diagonals.
