Square-Mirror Sum Generatork Terms

Created by Inder J. Taneja

Find k numbers whose squares add up to the same total as the squares of their reversals.

n₁² + n₂² + ··· + nₖ² = rev(n₁)² + rev(n₂)² + ··· + rev(nₖ)²
Example: 14² + 87² = 41² + 78² = 7765

Searches: n₁²+n₂²+...+nₖ² = rev(n₁)²+rev(n₂)²+...+rev(nₖ)²
