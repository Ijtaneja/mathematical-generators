Patterns in Single-Digit Representations – Generator

Search a starting number and grow its representations into verified families

Created by Inder J. Taneja

The search combines a general structural engine with a verified catalogue of manually discovered families. Repeated-digit blocks, synchronized growth and independent growth are inferred automatically; every displayed identity is then checked exactly.

Individual single-digit representations

Concatenation, basic operations, and exponential powers. Factorials are excluded.

The generator is based on the author’s following two works:
1. Inder J. Taneja, Single Digit Representations of Natural Numbers, February 2015, pp. 1–55, arXiv:1502.03501.
2. Inder J. Taneja, Single Digit Representations of Natural Numbers From 1 to 5000, Zenodo, January 14, 2019, doi:10.5281/zenodo.2538893.
All identities are checked with exact rational arithmetic. “No result” means no family was found inside the selected finite structural search—not that no mathematical pattern exists.
