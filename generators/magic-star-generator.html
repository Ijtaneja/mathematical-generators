Magic Star Generator

Solutions and Diagrams — exact search

n-pointed star {n/2} · n outer points and n inner corners · every straight line of four numbers has the same sum

Created by Inder J. Taneja

A magic star has n outer points and n inner corners. Each of its n straight lines holds four numbers: two outer points and two inner corners. Every number lies on exactly two lines, so adding all lines gives:
n × M = 2 × (sum of all the numbers)   →   with 1 to 2n: M = 4n + 2
The exact search finds every star or proves that none exists. Rotations and reflections count once. For five points the numbers 1–10 give no star, but 1–12 with two numbers left out can work — choose “leave two out”.
