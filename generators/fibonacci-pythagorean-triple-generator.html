Fibonacci → Pythagorean Triple Generator

Any four consecutive terms of a Fibonacci-style sequence form a Pythagorean Triple
Created by Inder J. Taneja
For four consecutive terms Gₙ, Gₙ₊₁, Gₙ₊₂, Gₙ₊₃ of any sequence obeying Gₙ₊₂ = Gₙ₊₁ + Gₙ, the following triple is constructed: a = Gₙ·Gₙ₊₃, b = 2·Gₙ₊₁·Gₙ₊₂, c = Gₙ₊₁² + Gₙ₊₂², and it always satisfies a² + b² = c².
