A single seed A grows each step, and the pattern detectors run on A² (A×A) instead of a product of separate factors. Three pattern types are checked: substring (result contains previous result), insertion (same digits inserted at regular positions each step), and palindrome (A² itself reads the same forwards and backwards at every step — the classic case being repunits: 11²=121, 111²=12321, 1111²=1234321...).
