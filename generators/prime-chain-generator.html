How it works. The search follows every branch level by level and reports exact totals, the longest chains and where the tree splits most. All arithmetic is exact: primes up to 3.3 × 10²⁴ are proven prime; longer ones pass 13 Miller–Rabin rounds. Examples: from 7, adding on the right ends at 73939133 (8 digits), on the left at 24 digits, on either side at 31 digits. Growing inside never seems to stop — use the limits and Stop.
