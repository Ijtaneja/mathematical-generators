Equal-Sum 14x14 Block Magic Squares Generator

Multiples of 14 - 42 verified choices through order 108 - Six block structures

Options

The complete magic square uses 1 through n*n exactly once and is divided into equal-sum 14x14 magic blocks. Choose plain, fully concentric, or 12+2 bordered blocks with the selected inner division. The 3x4 option has equal totals and equal row sums; its columns need not have equal sums.

Magic Square 8x8 Pandiagonal

Equal-sum block and inner-structure verification

Every selected block, core and inner division is verified according to its stated property. The complete square is also checked for equal rows, columns and both main diagonals.
