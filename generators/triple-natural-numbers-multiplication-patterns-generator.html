Three factors x, y, z grow together each step (x + gap1 = y, y + gap2 = z by default), and the pattern detectors run on the triple product x×y×z instead of the pair tool's x×y.

Mirror-style products — every factor reversed

Compare x×y×z with rev(x)×rev(y)×rev(z), inserting the same digit into every factor at each step. Reversed product: the reversed factors give the reversed product (12×13 = 156 and 21×31 = 651); this always happens when no column of the multiplication carries. Equal product: the reversed factors give the same product (12×42 = 21×24 = 504).

Mirror interval — automatic search
