Two streaming passes avoid storing the full square. Exact BigInt arithmetic protects large sums. Large files can take several minutes. Repeated entries are checked independently of magic, bimagic and trimagic sums. Upload the extracted TXT matrix, rather than a ZIP or numbered archive part. What is checked?

Magic: all rows, columns and both main diagonals have one common sum. Bimagic: the square is magic and the same lines also have a common sum after every entry is squared. Trimagic: all these conditions hold and the same lines have a common cube sum.

Pandiagonal: the square is magic and all n wraparound diagonals in each direction have that common sum. Pandiagonal through power 2: bimagic sum tests and all wraparound sums pass for powers 1 and 2. Pandiagonal through power 3: trimagic sum tests and all wraparound sums pass for powers 1, 2 and 3. A trimagic square need not be pandiagonal.

Wraparound ↘ line k + 1 has c − r ≡ k (mod n); wraparound ↙ line k + 1 has c + r ≡ k (mod n), with zero-based r and c. Main diagonals are shown separately as ↘ and ↙.
