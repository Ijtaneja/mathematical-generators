Fixed Digit-Split Repetitions Prime Patterns

Created by Inder J. Taneja

Split a fixed digit block into at least two parts and insert those parts at different positions of a prime seed. Repeat each part at its own position. For example, block 21 split as 2 | 1 with seed 13 gives 13 → 2131 → 221311 → 22213111.

Choose the search

The block is split into consecutive parts, retaining its digit order. Each part goes into a different gap of the seed; gaps can be at the front, between seed digits, or at the end. For 3d–9d, the suggested starting range is 100 blocks. Set the two block limits equal to search one block. A 2d value 7 is treated as 07.

How many split parts?

Enough distinct insertion gaps must exist in the seed.
