Fixed-Digit Repetition Prime Patterns Generator

Created by Inder J. Taneja

Choose a prime seed and a fixed digit block. Insert one, then two, then three copies of that same block at the chosen position. For a seed AB, the sequence is AB → A(k)B → A(kk)B → A(kkk)B. The whole block is repeated; its individual digits may repeat.

Choose the search

A 1d range of 1–9 tests 9 blocks; a 2d range of 10–99 tests 90 blocks. For 3d–9d, the suggested starting range contains 100 blocks; widen it as needed. Set Block from and Block to to the same number to test one block. Leading zeros are allowed: enter 7–7 with 2d to test 07. At the front, an inserted number must not begin with 0.

Prime run

“Exactly” verifies that the next insertion is composite. “At least” continues through the prime run and then shows its first composite term separately (up to 30 prime terms).
