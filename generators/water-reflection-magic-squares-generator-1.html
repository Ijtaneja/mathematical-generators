Water Reflection, Upside-Down & Mirror Looking Magic Squares Generator

Order 3–25. Choose the square order, the allowed digital digits, and the number of digits in every cell. Choose water reflection, upside-down, or all three: water reflection, upside-down and mirror looking. The results verify the selected row, column and diagonal conditions; Excel places the original and transformed squares side by side.

Created by Inder J. Taneja
0, 1, 8 stay the same in a mirror; 2 and 5 swap with each other. (3, 4, 6, 7, 9 have no real mirror image.)
Zero normally appears only in an interior position. For 7×7 two-digit Upside-down squares, fixed-width entries such as 01 are preserved so all 49 combinations are available.
