Multiple Blocks Magic Squares Generator

Multiples of orders 3x3 to 14x14

Options

Click a block size, then a block structure, then an order, then a sum type. Each square uses 1 through n*n exactly once; each choice states whether the block sums are equal or different, the block type, and whether the complete square is pandiagonal. Every block, core and inner division is verified.

Block and inner-structure verification

Every selected block, core and inner division is verified according to its stated property. The complete square is also checked for equal rows, columns and both main diagonals.
